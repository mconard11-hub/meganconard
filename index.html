About Me

Hi, I’m Megan!

Every job I’ve had has come back to energy, from refinery turnarounds at Phillips 66 to carbon capture membranes at Saint-Gobain to now financing energy transition projects.

I hold a degree in Mechanical Engineering from the University of Colorado Boulder and spent two years at Saint-Gobain, first as a research engineer on energy and sustainability technology and then on sustainability process solutions for gypsum board.

Along the way, I saw technical projects stall because the numbers didn’t work or nobody made the financial case. While engineering decides what can be built, finance decides what will get built.

As an engineer, I saw how infrastructure projects get deployed. My MSc in Energy Management, with a specialization in Finance and Investment, at ESCP Business School showed me where the money that builds them comes from.
