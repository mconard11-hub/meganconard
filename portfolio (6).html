About Me

Engineer.
Strategist.
Energy Transition.

I hold a degree in Mechanical Engineering with a Minor in Energy Engineering from the University of Colorado Boulder and am currently completing an MSc in Energy Management with a specialization in Finance and Investment at ESCP Business School.

This is a combination that has been deliberate from the beginning. While working in engineering, I have come to understand that investment decisions in energy require both financial rigor and a clear understanding of the operational drivers that underpin asset performance. Without this, it doesn't matter how good the technical solution — a project will end.

I have always believed that energy is the foundation of progress. It enables innovation and growth, while also shaping the challenges of the energy transition.
